CLM-218 | Reject incomplete synthetic claims submissions

Current behavior: a dictionary is accepted even when a required field is missing.
Required fields: claim_ref and loss_date. Both must be non-blank strings.
Complete submissions still return 202 and {"status": "accepted"}.
Missing, blank or non-string required fields must return 400 and
{"error": "missing_required_fields"}. Non-object input keeps its existing error.

Out of scope: date parsing, field normalization, real claims data, external services
and the separate audit-marker finding. Keep the regression tests unchanged.
